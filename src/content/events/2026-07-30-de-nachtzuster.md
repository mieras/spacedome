Space Dome is more than a party; it's a tribute to the low-end theory of the underground LA beat scene. Inspired by the legendary nights at Melkweg Amsterdam, we brought the bass of the West Coast beat scene to De Nachtzuster in Tilburg.

Trip-hop, beats, deep grooves and experimental rhythms. A long soundtrack from J Dilla to Flying Lotus, from Massive Attack to DJ Shadow. No rush, just hypnotic bass and a close-knit vibe.
